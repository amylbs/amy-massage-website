About me

Hi, I'm Amy. Movement has always been a big part of my life. I grew up skiing and playing team sports in the Colorado mountains, and transitioned to triathlons and open water swimming. These days I'm all about functional strength training and keeping my body healthy and injury-free.

I'm endlessly curious about how the body works and what helps it perform at its best. That curiosity brought me to massage and bodywork, where I help others release built-up tension and bring ease back into their body.
